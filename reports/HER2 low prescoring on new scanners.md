# Promote the cell engine, calibrate per site

BioMarkHER2 should stop treating its attention-MIL head as the HER2 pre-scorer at new sites. It should make the per-cell ASCO/CAP rule engine the primary 0/1+/2+/3+ call, run it on tumour-masked tissue, and recalibrate its two intensity and completeness cut-points per site against on-slide controls or a small set of locally scored slides. Everything in the literature that holds up at external sites for 1+ and 2+ follows this pattern: cell-level membrane quantification reported as percentages of cells per class, plus site or assay calibration and pathologist review near thresholds. Nothing in the notes shows a slide-label-trained patch classifier doing the same. Your own measurements point the same way. Macenko normalisation changed nothing, slide-label fine-tuning made results worse, and the rule engine got the 1+ slide right while the AI called it 0. Expectations need to be realistic. Ten independent commercial and academic HER2 models scoring the same 1,124 slides agreed only 65.1% of the time (kappa 0.51), against 97.3% for 3+ versus not-3+, and agreement was lowest for HER2-low ([McKelvey et al., Mod Pathol 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.modpat.2025.100944)). The aim is therefore pathologist-level agreement at each clinical cut-off, not perfection. A few days of work gets the system to an interpretable, calibratable cell-level pre-score with review flags. Proving that it works needs more 1+/2+ slides than HER2match has: with one 1+ and one 2+ held-out slide, every conclusion so far rests on n=1.

## The system and the failure this plan targets

BioMarkHER2 is a ResNet-50 multi-task U-Net with a Lunit self-supervised encoder. It was trained on HER2-IHC-40x (region/patch labels) and BCI, and a gated-attention MIL head pools tumour tiles into a slide pre-score. A separate threshold-based rule engine measures membrane completeness and intensity per cell and applies the ASCO/CAP 10% rules. The system already has a whole-slide pipeline, a tumour-region marking tool, an on-slide control measurement hook (control DAB gain), a stain-profile normalisation option, and RunPod fine-tuning scripts. On HER2match whole-slide images (3DHistech P1000, 0.25 µm/px, one score per slide), it calls 0 and 3+ correctly but calls the held-out 1+ and 2+ slides 0.

This week's measurements on 4 held-out HER2match slides (1,556 tumour tiles) were:

| Experiment | Result |
|---|---|
| Baseline tile accuracy | 56.7% |
| Macenko normalisation to the training site | 56.8% (no change); pooled DAB 99th-percentile concentration 1.54 vs 1.56 at the training site |
| Fine-tuning on HER2match with slide-level labels (6 and 13 slides, with and without normalisation) | Worse: 35–55% tile accuracy; one 0 slide over-called as 2+ |
| Pathologist-marked tumour region + per-cell rule engine | 1+ slide called 1+ (AI still said 0); 2+ slide called 0 by both |

Each recommendation below is tied to one of these observations.

## Why 1+ and 2+ break across sites while 0 and 3+ survive

### The categories are perceptual thresholds sitting on top of a several-fold assay spread

The ASCO/CAP 2023 wording is relative and perceptual. **1+ is "incomplete membrane staining that is faint/barely perceptible and in >10% of tumour cells"** and **2+ is "weak to moderate complete membrane staining observed in >10% of tumour cells"**, while 3+ must be "readily appreciated using a low-power objective" ([NordiQC B41, 2026](https://nordiqc.org/downloads/assessments/215_11.pdf)). None of these is tied to a physical unit. A shift in DAB optical density from the assay, the lab or the scanner therefore moves cases across the 0/1+ and 1+/2+ lines while leaving 3+ and true 0 where they were.

That shift is large. When 35 Dutch labs stained identical calibrator material and the same AI measured it, **a 1+ control core read anywhere from 1.6% to 71.3% positive cells** (median 4.3%), and the 2+ core ranged from 30.4% to 92.6% ([Hempenius et al., Histopathology 2024](https://doi.org/10.1111/his.15273)). In CASI-01, labs running the same unmodified PATHWAY 4B5 kit showed a **36% inter-laboratory CV in limit of detection** (30,000–60,000 molecules/cell). Image analysis picked up that difference with roughly a six-fold steeper slope than pathologists did ([Dabbs et al., eBioMedicine 2025](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12571579/)). In the latest NordiQC run, **64% of non-optimal stains failed because a HER2-low case changed category, mainly toward negative**. Its confusion table also shows a large 1+↔2+ swap: for NordiQC-1+ cores, 241 participant reads were 2+, and for NordiQC-2+ cores, 152 were 1+ ([NordiQC B41](https://nordiqc.org/downloads/assessments/215_11.pdf)). Under-calling HER2-low as 0 is the most frequently documented cross-lab failure, which is the same direction as BioMarkHER2's error.

### Ground truth at these boundaries is itself noisy

Only **26% of 92 cases reached ≥90% agreement on 0 vs 1+ among 18 pathologists**, compared with 58% for 2+ vs 3+ ([Fernandez et al., JAMA Oncol 2022, via ASCO Post](https://ascopost.com/news/february-2022/concordance-for-low-her2-protein-expression-in-breast-cancer-tissue-using-standard-immunohistochemistry-assays)). In a 45-lab ring study, 1+ concordance was 57.6% (kappa 0.566) ([Dong et al., NPJ Breast Cancer 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41523-025-00872-2)). When one 53-case TMA was stained in 35 labs, **84.8% of cases called 1+ in some lab were called 0 in another** ([Yang et al., Gland Surg 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12261348/)). A single HER2match slide label is one reading of one section. Some apparent model "errors" at a new site are therefore disagreements between labels, not failures of the model.

### Your four measurements diagnose the specific failure

**Stain normalisation could not have helped, and the similar DAB percentile does not clear intensity as the cause.** Macenko rescales each image's stain concentrations to a reference percentile, so by design it does not preserve absolute DAB intensity, which is the quantity the 0/1+/2+ rules depend on. In an H-DAB study, Macenko and histogram matching both "failed to sufficiently highlight" DAB regions ([EpidermaQuant, arXiv 2406.03103](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.03103)). No HER2-specific paper measures how Macenko shifts 0/1+ calls, so this remains an inference. The 1.54 vs 1.56 match in pooled 99th-percentile DAB mostly reflects the brightest membranes, which come from 3+ slides. It says nothing about where faint and weak-to-moderate membranes fall relative to the rule engine's cut-offs. Answering that requires per-cell membrane OD distributions measured on the 1+ and 2+ slides themselves.

**Slide-label fine-tuning failed for structural reasons.** A slide is 1+ if more than 10% of tumour cells show faint incomplete staining, so most tiles on a genuine 1+ slide can look like 0. Within-tissue HER2 heterogeneity reaches five-fold ([Aidt et al., J Pathol Inform 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpi.2025.100513)). Copying the slide label onto every tile injects label noise exactly where the signal is weakest. Doing that with 6–13 slides teaches the encoder those slides' identities (inference). Weakly supervised HER2 MIL is fragile under shift even on H&E, falling from 83.3% in-domain to 53.8% on an external set ([INESC TEC](https://repositorio.inesctec.pt/handle/123456789/11438)). The same argument means the 56.7% tile-accuracy figure is not a valid metric either, because a tile from a 1+ slide that the model calls 0 may well be correct.

**The rule engine succeeding on 1+ where the AI failed locates the fault in pooling, not perception.** Given the same pathologist-marked tumour region, the cell-level measurement found enough faint, incomplete membranes to cross the >10% line. The attention head instead voted with the majority-0 tiles. This matches how successful systems are built. Lunit trained separate tissue and cell models on 1,133–1,259 HER2 slides with 7.31×10⁵ annotated tumour cells ([Jung et al., Breast Cancer Res 2024](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13058-024-01784-y); [ASCO abstract](https://ace.asco.org/content/373468/abstract/212306)), and Mindpeak and Roche report percentages of cells per class inside a tumour region ([Roche uPath HER2](https://diagnostics.roche.com/gb/en/products/digital/upath-her2-4b5-image-analysis-breast-pid00000378.html); [Mindpeak ASCO 2023](https://ace.asco.org/content/404656/abstract/221625)).

**The 2+ slide failing on both paths points to an engine threshold or a data problem, and has to be diagnosed before it is fixed.** The notes support four hypotheses, in descending order of how cheap they are to test. First, the "weak-to-moderate" intensity cut or the completeness criterion may be set too strictly for this scanner and assay, so complete 2+ membranes are binned as faint, incomplete or 0. This fits the dominant too-weak failure mode in NordiQC. Second, a resolution mismatch: HER2match is 0.25 µm/px ([Zenodo 15797050](https://zenodo.org/records/15797050)), and if membrane ring width or completeness sampling was tuned at a different pixel size, a thin membrane can be read as broken. Third, HER2match IHC was performed on the same section after H&E ([Zenodo 15797050](https://zenodo.org/records/15797050)), which may weaken or alter DAB relative to a routine section. This is an untested hypothesis, not a finding. Fourth, the one-score-per-slide label may describe the case rather than this re-stained section. The test that separates these is a per-cell scatter of membrane OD against completeness for the 0, 1+, 2+ and 3+ slides, plus a look at the overlays. If the 2+ cells form a cloud that sits between 1+ and 3+ but under the current cut, the cause is calibration. If there is no such cloud, the cause is the data or the section.

## Methods with external evidence for 1+/2+, ranked for BioMarkHER2

The evidence has an uneven shape. Strong multi-site results exist for cell-level AI as an aid to pathologists, and for physical calibration on a small scale. Domain-generalisation tricks have H&E-only evidence. Foundation models have strong in-domain HER2 numbers but documented scanner fragility.

| Rank | Method | External 1+/2+ evidence | Effort in BioMarkHER2 | Labels needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cell-level rule engine as primary pre-score, tumour-masked, with % cells per class output | Strongest (multi-site reader studies, vendor designs) | 0.5–1 day (already built) | None new |
| 2 | Per-site calibration of intensity/completeness cut-points (on-slide control, else 10–30 local slides) | Direct HER2 evidence, small scale (Ohnishi); assay-spread evidence (Hempenius, CASI-01) | 1–3 days | Controls or 10–30 scored slides |
| 3 | Borderline flagging near the 10% and intensity cut-offs | Discordance clusters at 10% cut (Deman); FDA re-read zone 1–5% | 0.5 day | None |
| 4 | Rebuild the MIL head: tumour masking, rule-consistent/ordinal pooling, region labels and rule-engine pseudo-labels instead of slide labels | Indirect (heterogeneity, weak-supervision fragility) | 2–3 days | Region labels (HER2-IHC-40x) |
| 5 | Nuisance-only stain augmentation + AdaBN/FUSION at inference | H&E only; none for HER2 1+/2+ | 1 day | None |
| 6 | Foundation-model features (H-optimus-0) with leave-one-site-out | In-domain kappa 0.86; FMs encode scanner/centre | 2+ days, medium term | Region labels |

### Cell-level quantification is what holds up at external sites

AI tools that output cell-level metrics raise reader agreement most at exactly the classes BioMarkHER2 misses. In Lunit's external hospital set, **1+ agreement among three pathologists rose from 26.5% to 70.7% and 2+ from 46.2% to 68.4%** with AI ([Jung et al., Breast Cancer Res 2024](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13058-024-01784-y)). Ibex's four-site study raised 0-vs-1+ agreement from 69.8% to 87.4% ([Krishnamurthy et al., JCO Precis Oncol 2024](https://cris.tau.ac.il/en/publications/fully-automated-artificial-intelligence-solution-for-human-epider/)). In a 15-pathologist, five-institution study, cell-level membrane AI lifted accuracy on heterogeneous cases from 0.68 to 0.89 ([Wu et al., Mod Pathol 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.modpat.2022.100054)). Mindpeak, the vendor most explicit about scanner diversity, validated across 4 institutions and 5 scanners. Fully automatic WSI scoring reached 86–89% agreement for HER2-negative vs low/positive, but only 78.7% four-class agreement, and these figures are from a conference abstract ([JCO 2023;41(16_suppl):516](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2023.41.16_suppl.516)).

Two caveats temper this. Stand-alone cell-level systems are only moderately accurate: Visiopharm's HER2 app matched expert consensus in 73.5% of cases (Fleiss kappa 0.433), rising to kappa 0.860 on high-agreement HER2-low cases ([Parry et al., J Pathol Clin Res 2025](https://doi.org/10.1002/2056-4538.70051); [Birmingham record](https://research.birmingham.ac.uk/en/publications/interrater-agreement-of-her2low-scores-between-expert-breast-path/)). Mindpeak's AI on IHC reached Cohen's kappa 0.59, with most errors between 0 and 1+ ([Palm et al., Diagnostics 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9818571/)). Also, almost every cited gain is AI-assisted reading, not autonomous scoring. Both caveats argue for positioning BioMarkHER2's output as a pre-score with cell-level evidence and review flags, which is also the regulatory posture: the FDA-cleared HER2 tools are assistive and "the pathologist remains the ultimate authority" ([BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/ventana-medical-systems-inc-wins-fda-clearance-for-cancer-detection-device)).

### Calibration against a physical reference is the only intervention with direct HER2 cross-scanner evidence

In the MSKCC protocol, a colour-chart slide calibrated the scanner and a peptide-microbead calibrator slide (10 concentrations) calibrated DAB staining. With both in place, **automated HER2 scoring agreed with the pathologist 100% across two scanners and stain batches**, while results varied without them ([Ohnishi et al., Appl Microsc 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10499734/)). The study is small and its sample size was not extracted, so treat it as proof of principle. The design logic is sound, though, because scanner colour and assay sensitivity vary independently and each needs its own anchor. NordiQC recommends a 1+/2+/3+ control TMA on the same slide as patient tissue ([NordiQC](https://www.nordiqc.org/downloads/assessments/193_11.pdf)), and ASCO/CAP 2023 asks for controls spanning low expression ([CAP guideline page](https://www.cap.org/protocols-and-guidelines/cap-guidelines/current-cap-guidelines/recommendations-for-human-epidermal-growth-factor-2-testing-in-breast-cancer)). BioMarkHER2's control DAB-gain hook is therefore the right lever. The change needed is to express the rule engine's cut-offs as multiples of the control's measured 1+ and 2+ membrane OD instead of absolute OD.

Where no controls are present (HER2match has none), the fallback is to refit the cut-points on a few locally scored slides. Thresholds clearly do not transfer: one cohort's optimal 6.13% weak-cell cut-off for ultralow vs null reached 81% accuracy there ([Hamadouche et al., Virchows Arch 2026](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00428-026-04504-3)), and the Hempenius spread is larger than the whole 0-to-2+ range. Calibration has a hard ceiling. If a site's assay detection limit lies above a tumour's HER2 level, faint 1+ never reaches the glass ([CASI-01](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12571579/)), and no image processing recovers it.

### Continuous read-outs and threshold-aware flags beat hard four-class calls

AI continuous HER2 read-outs correlate with calibrated quantitative IHC at **r=0.94** in IHC 0/1+ tumours ([Aidt et al., J Pathol Inform 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpi.2025.100513)). This supports keeping membrane OD and cell-class percentages as the internal signal and deriving categories from them, which makes per-site recalibration a matter of moving cut-points. AI-assisted discordances "clustered around the 10% ASCO/CAP cutoff", driven mainly by under-calling of 2+ ([Deman et al., Lab Invest 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.labinv.2026.106150)). The 4B5 CDx label sends 1–5% staining cases to adjudication by one or two extra pathologists ([FDA SSED P990081/S055](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf/P990081S055B.pdf)). Together these justify a pre-score that reports its distance to each cut-off and abstains near one.

### The MIL head is worth rebuilding, not fine-tuning harder

No paper directly compares ordinal or rule-consistent MIL with free attention MIL for HER2 IHC, so this ranking rests on mechanism, not head-to-head evidence. Three changes follow from the failure analysis. First, tumour-mask the bag so that DCIS, normal ducts and edge artefact are excluded. Second, add a head that predicts per-tile class fractions and computes the slide score deterministically from the 10% rule, keeping attention as a parallel output. Third, replace the four-class softmax with an ordinal cumulative-link output whose cut-points can be refit on 5–20 local slides while the network stays frozen. An ordinal Wasserstein Dice loss has been used for HER2 membrane-class segmentation (F1 0.832) ([Öttl et al., J Pathol Inform 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpi.2025.100435)). Train only on region labels (HER2-IHC-40x) plus rule-engine pseudo-labels from confidently scored slides, never on slide labels copied to tiles. Published slide-label approaches top out around F1 83.5% for three-way HER2, with HER2-0 weakest at 75.6% ([Chauhan et al., J Pathol Inform 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpi.2025.100529)).

### Augmentation, test-time adaptation and foundation models are secondary

HED-space augmentation gave the strongest cross-centre gains in a pre-2021 H&E benchmark ([Tellez et al., Med Image Anal 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.media.2019.101544)), and RandStainNA beat augmentation or normalisation alone ([Shen et al., MICCAI 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.12694)). Neither has been tested on HER2 1+/2+. For IHC there is a trap: DAB intensity *is* the label, so aggressive DAB-magnitude jitter teaches the model to ignore the 0/1+ signal. Jitter hue, haematoxylin and saturation freely and DAB magnitude only modestly (inference). FUSION-style BatchNorm blending recomputes encoder statistics on unlabelled target tiles without changing pixels ([Chattopadhyay et al., arXiv 2208.14206](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.14206v1)). It is cheap for a ResNet-50, but a 2,958-run TTA benchmark found that CNNs are prone to "silent failures" ([Bahumanya et al., arXiv 2608.07062](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07062)), so check it against labelled slides and skip entropy-minimisation methods like TENT.

Foundation models give good in-domain HER2 scorers: Virchow reached linear weighted kappa 0.860 on 66 test slides ([Wang et al., Diagnostics 2025](https://doi.org/10.3390/diagnostics15172131)). However, all 10 public pathology FMs tested "represent the medical center to a strong degree" ([arXiv 2501.18055](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18055)), and across 14 FMs, embeddings and calibration failed across scanners ([Thiringer et al., arXiv 2601.04163](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.04163)). A cross-scanner kappa of 0.97 from a Virchow2 plus cell-topology system put all its errors at 1+ vs 2+ ([Frontiers Public Health 2026](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/public-health/articles/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1940487/full)), but per-class data were not checked, so treat it as an outlier. An FM swap does not replace calibration. If you try one, H-optimus-0 is Apache 2.0, while UNI2-h, Virchow2 and H-optimus-1 are non-commercial ([HF H-optimus-1](https://huggingface.co/bioptimus/H-optimus-1); [model list](https://github.com/georg-wolflein/pathology-foundation-models)).

## A five-day recipe using public data

The recipe keeps the existing architecture, puts the rule engine first, and uses public sets for leave-one-site-out development. The available public HER2 IHC domains are HER2-IHC-40x (107 WSIs, ROI-level labels, CC-BY-SA; patch counts 3,789/2,153/634/3,364 for 0/1+/2+/3+, so 2+ is only about 6%) ([Zenodo 15179607](https://zenodo.org/records/15179607)), ADEL (KFB PRO 120 scanner, Kazakhstan, image-level scores with ISH for equivocal cases, CC-BY 4.0) ([Zenodo 15872690](https://zenodo.org/records/15872690)), HER2match (17 WSI pairs, 3DHistech, CC-BY-NC-SA) ([Zenodo 15797050](https://zenodo.org/records/15797050)), and the Warwick contest set (registration required). Warwick's ground truth includes the percentage of cells with complete membrane staining, so it can directly calibrate the completeness measure ([arXiv 1705.08369](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1705.08369)). No public HER2 set has cell-level membrane annotations. HER2match and BCI carry non-commercial terms ([BCI](https://bupt-ai-cz.github.io/BCI/)), so keep them out of anything shipped commercially.

| Day | Work | Output / decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Diagnose 2+.** Dump per-cell (membrane OD, completeness, nucleus confidence) for all HER2match slides; plot by slide label; overlay the classified cells on the 2+ slide at 40x. Check that the engine resamples to its tuned µm/px at 0.25 µm/px. Run the AI MIL head inside the same pathologist region to separate pooling error from region error. | Cause of 2+ miss: threshold, resolution, cell detection, or data/section |
| 1 | **Switch the pre-score.** Make the tumour-masked rule-engine call the primary output. Emit % cells complete-strong / complete-weak-moderate / incomplete-faint / any, median membrane OD, and the AI call as a secondary concordance check. | Interpretable pre-score; AI/engine disagreement as a flag |
| 2 | **Borderline flags.** Flag slides whose qualifying-cell fraction is within ±3 percentage points of 10%, whose dominant cells fall within a band around an intensity cut, whose %TC is 1–5% (FDA re-read zone), or where AI and engine disagree. | "Review" label instead of a confident wrong call |
| 2–3 | **Per-site calibration.** If a control is on the slide, use the control DAB-gain hook to rescale the faint and weak-moderate OD cuts relative to the control's measured 1+/2+ membranes. Otherwise grid-search the faint OD cut, weak-moderate OD cut and completeness threshold, maximising agreement at the 0/≥1+ and ≤1+/≥2+ cuts on a locked calibration subset (HER2match dev slides, ADEL); fit completeness against Warwick % complete. Store thresholds per scanner/site profile. | Site profile file; thresholds locked before testing |
| 3–4 | **Rebuild the MIL head (optional in this sprint).** Tumour-masked bags; rule-consistent fraction head plus ordinal cumulative-link head; train on HER2-IHC-40x region labels with 2+ oversampling, plus rule-engine pseudo-labels on confident public slides; nuisance-only stain augmentation (hue/H/saturation, mild DAB magnitude). Refit ordinal cut-points per site on 5–20 slides. | AI head that agrees with the rule and recalibrates cheaply |
| 4 | **AdaBN/FUSION check.** Recompute encoder BN stats on 200–1,000 unlabelled target tiles; blend α∈{0.3, 0.5, 0.7}; keep only if 1+/2+ calls improve without drift on labelled slides. | Go/no-go |
| 5 | **Leave-one-site-out evaluation** (protocol below) across HER2-IHC-40x, ADEL, HER2match and Warwick. | Per-site report; decision on whether to request local slides |

The plan drops three things: further Macenko or global stain-normalisation tuning, more slide-label fine-tuning on HER2match, and replacing the backbone before calibration is done.

## An evaluation protocol that can actually detect 1+/2+ progress

Stop reporting tile accuracy against slide labels, because a correct 0 tile on a 1+ slide is counted as an error. The primary unit is the slide. Report a 4×4 confusion matrix (5×5 once ultralow is scored), per-class sensitivity and PPV, and positive, negative and overall agreement at each binary clinical cut-off: 0 vs ≥1+ (HER2-low eligibility), ≤1+ vs ≥2+ (ISH reflex), and ≤2+ vs 3+. Use case-level percentile bootstrap CIs with 2,000 resamples and Wilson intervals for 100% results, as in the 4B5 CDx submission ([FDA SSED P990081/S055](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf/P990081S055B.pdf)). Report quadratic-weighted kappa alongside unweighted kappa, because weighting under-penalises the one-step 0↔1+ errors that matter clinically. This recommendation is an inference, not a cited standard. Report abstention rate and accuracy on non-flagged slides separately, so that flagging cannot hide errors.

Development uses leave-one-site-out across the public domains, with thresholds fitted only on calibration slides and locked before the held-out site is scored. HER2match's 17 slides can serve as a calibration and smoke-test set for 3DHistech, not as validation. A per-class sensitivity of about 90% measured on 20 slides already carries a 95% CI half-width of roughly ±13%, so a single 1+ slide shows nothing either way.

Before trusting a real new hospital, use an SSED-style local verification on that site's stainer, clone and scanner. Take about 100–120 slides, roughly 25 null, 20 ultralow, 25 1+, 25 2+ with ISH, and 15 3+, enriched for near-threshold and heterogeneous cases. The reference should be a glass-slide consensus of at least 3 breast pathologists with a predefined adjudication rule. The case mix is a synthesis, not a guideline number. It is anchored to the FDA precision design of 100 cases (20 borderline), 3 readers, 2 reads and a 2-week washout, which reached 95.3% between-reader agreement at the eligibility cut (CI 92.0–98.0) ([FDA SSED](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf/P990081S055B.pdf)). It also follows CAP rules: at least 90% overall concordance and separate verification for each assay and scoring-system combination ([CAP IHC validation 2024](https://www.cap.org/protocols-and-guidelines/cap-guidelines/current-cap-guidelines/principles-of-analytic-validation-of-immunohistochemical-assays)), and at least 60 cases with concern below 95% WSI-to-glass concordance for the scanner leg ([Evans et al., Arch Pathol Lab Med 2022](https://meridian.allenpress.com/aplm/article-pdf/146/4/440/3030324/i1543-2165-146-4-440.pdf)).

The acceptance test should be non-inferiority of AI-vs-reader agreement against reader-vs-reader agreement at each cut-off, following PathAI's nested pairwise design ([PathAI](https://www.pathai.com/publications/analysis-of-pathologists-intraobserver-interobserver-and-ai-agreement-in-breast-cancer-her2-ihc-scoring)), plus a hard rule that no 3+ or ISH-amplified case is called ≤1+. Name the reference panel in advance and report agreement with each member, because "measurements of model accuracy are highly dependent on reference panel composition" ([Shamshoian et al., medRxiv 2025](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=DOI:10.1101/2025.02.04.25321335&format=json&resultType=core)). After go-live, re-verify after any scanner, antibody lot or software change. Monitor the class distribution against baseline, track per-pathologist override rates, and scan a control TMA daily, in line with the CAP QIA guideline's ongoing-QC requirement ([CAP QIA guideline](https://www.cap.org/protocols-and-guidelines/cap-guidelines/current-cap-guidelines/quantitative-image-analysis-of-her2-immunohistochemistry-for-breast-cancer)). Numeric drift triggers are not specified anywhere in the sources.

## Conclusion

The 1+ result already shows where the fix lies. The measurement layer (cells, membranes, OD) transfers to a new scanner well enough. What does not transfer is the decision layer: a learned pooling head that absorbed one site's sense of "faint", and fixed cut-points that encode one assay's sensitivity. That moves the engineering problem from domain generalisation of a network to calibration of a few interpretable numbers per site, which takes days, can be audited, and is what regulators and pathologists already accept. It also changes what counts as the product. A pre-score that says "1+, 14% faint incomplete cells, 4 points above the cut-off, calibrated to this lab's control" can be trusted at a new site in a way that a bare class label cannot.

What remains open is real. No published study isolates "trained at site A, 1+ called 0 at site B" with a scanner/stain ablation, and none tests Macenko's effect on HER2 0/1+. BioMarkHER2's leave-one-site-out results could fill that gap. The 2+ failure stays unexplained until the per-cell scatter is examined, and it may turn out to be a HER2match section or label artefact rather than a model fault. The binding constraint after this sprint is labelled local slides at 1+ and 2+, not methods. The most valuable request to a partner hospital is about 25 scored 1+ and 25 scored 2+ slides with on-slide controls.
